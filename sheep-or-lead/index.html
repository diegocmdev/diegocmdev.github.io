The flock fights back.

Not this time, wolf.

For generations, the foxes, wolves and coyotes have come for the flock. This sheep has decided enough is enough.

Sheep or Lead is a fast, bite-sized shooter made for your phone: the enemies keep coming, the clock keeps running, and every one you stop adds to your count. One more round is always just a tap away.

Watch the trailer

What's in the barn

  • Endless packs

    Foxes, wolves and coyotes come at you from both sides. Keep the clock running and the count going up.

  • A whole armoury

    Shotguns, assault rifles and more. Find the weapon that suits your style.

  • Dress for battle

    Bandanas, helmets, a feathered headdress… Every sheep deserves a look.

  • Three stages

    The windmill farm, the barn and the forest, each with its own hand-drawn backdrop.

  • Your difficulty

    Easy, medium or hard. Warm up on the farm or go straight for the hardest packs.

  • Unlock as you play

    Earn points to unlock new sheep, outfits and weapons. Available in English and Spanish.
