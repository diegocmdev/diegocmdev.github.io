Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

In short: Sheep or Lead does not require an account. Game settings and age category stay on your device. Advertising is intended only for adults, subject to applicable consent. Advertising providers may process device information as described below.

1. Who we are

Sheep or Lead (“the game”) is developed and published by PE Studio, an independent game studio run by Diego Ceballos in Spain (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for this policy and for the game.

Contact: diego.ceballos.dev@pestudio.eu

This policy applies to the Android version of the game, distributed through Google Play.

2. Information we collect

The game has no user accounts, chat or player content sharing. PE Studio does not operate a server collecting player profiles.

Your progress, settings and age category (adult or under 18/undisclosed) are stored only on your device. The game asks for your age to determine whether advertising may be enabled; it retains the category, not your exact age or date of birth. These local data can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and requests ads for eligible adults through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited. To show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob software inside the game may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be inferred;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information related to the ads.

This information is collected and used by Google, not by us. Google may use it to show personalized ads (based on your interests) only where you have given consent where it is required, or non-personalized ads (based on the general context, such as the game itself and your approximate location).

To learn more about how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

For adults in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game requests consent through Google UMP when required. If you reject advertising consent, the game disables ad requests. Adults can change their preferences from About → Manage consent, where available. Clearing app data also resets local settings and the age category. Advertising is not required to play.

How to limit ad tracking on your device

On most Android devices you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device).

4. Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, Google processes information about your download and your Google account under its own privacy policy. We only receive general statistics from Google Play, such as the number of installs, crash reports and ratings, which do not identify you.

5. Device permissions

The game uses internet access for advertising and web links, and includes advertising identifier and related Android advertising service permissions. The Android package also declares vibration, Bluetooth/controller support and legacy storage permissions. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise location permissions.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on consent (Article 6(1)(a)) for advertising processing requiring consent, including personalized advertising and access to device identifiers where legally required. You may withdraw consent at any time. Strictly necessary security and fraud prevention may rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), where applicable and subject to applicable safeguards. Google describes its own processing and legal bases in its privacy policy.

7. International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google applies safeguards recognized under the GDPR for these transfers, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Data retention

Local settings and the age category remain on your device until app data are cleared or the game is uninstalled. Google retains advertising data according to its own retention policies. If you contact us, we retain your correspondence as necessary to handle your request and comply with applicable obligations.

9. Children

The game contains cartoon violence and is intended for players aged 13 and over. The game is designed not to request advertising or advertising consent for players under 18 or those who decline to provide their age. All gameplay remains available without ads. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about your child's information, please contact us.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to it, to data portability and to withdraw your consent. For advertising data held by Google, requests can be directed to Google through your Google account settings. You can always contact us at diego.ceballos.dev@pestudio.eu and we will answer your request.

If you are in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Spain, this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es).

11. Security

Your game progress stays on your device. We do not operate servers that store information about players.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the game adds new features or services. We will publish any changes on this page and update the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know inside the game.
