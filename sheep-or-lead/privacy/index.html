Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

In short: Sheep or Lead does not ask you to create an account and we, PE Studio, do not collect or store your personal data. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data from your device to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

1. Who we are

Sheep or Lead (“the game”) is developed and published by PE Studio, an independent game studio run by Diego Ceballos in Spain (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for this policy and for the game.

Contact: diego.ceballos.dev@pestudio.eu

This policy applies to the Android version of the game, distributed through Google Play.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you. The game has no user accounts, no chat and no way to share content with other players.

Your progress and settings (for example, your points, unlocked items, difficulty and language) are saved only on your device. We cannot see them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited. To show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob software inside the game may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be inferred;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information related to the ads.

This information is collected and used by Google, not by us. Google may use it to show personalized ads (based on your interests) only where you have given consent where it is required, or non-personalized ads (based on the general context, such as the game itself and your approximate location).

To learn more about how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows you a consent message, provided by Google, the first time you open it. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads. If you do not give consent, you will only see non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the game's privacy options, if available, or by clearing the game's data in your Android settings, so that the consent message appears again.

How to limit ad tracking on your device

On most Android devices you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device).

4. Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, Google processes information about your download and your Google account under its own privacy policy. We only receive general statistics from Google Play, such as the number of installs, crash reports and ratings, which do not identify you.

5. Device permissions

The game uses the internet connection of your device to load ads, and the advertising ID permission used by AdMob. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files or precise location.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

Where the processing described in section 3 is subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), personalized advertising is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalized advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention are based on the legitimate interest of keeping the game free (Article 6(1)(f)).

7. International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google applies safeguards recognized under the GDPR for these transfers, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Data retention

We do not keep any personal data about you. Data collected by Google through AdMob is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

9. Children

The game contains cartoon violence and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help you.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to it, to data portability and to withdraw your consent. Since we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google through your Google account settings. You can always contact us at diego.ceballos.dev@pestudio.eu and we will answer your request.

If you are in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Spain, this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es).

11. Security

Your game progress stays on your device. We do not operate servers that store information about players.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the game adds new features or services. We will publish any changes on this page and update the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know inside the game.
