Website privacy & cookies

2026-11-28

Controller and scope

PE Studio — Diego Ceballos, Spain. Contact: diego.ceballos.dev@pestudio.eu. This notice covers pestudio.eu, not data processing inside the Sheep or Lead app, which has its own policy.

Website operation and contact

GitHub Pages hosts the website and may log IP addresses for security. Existing Google Fonts requests supply fonts and disclose technical connection information such as IP addresses to Google. If you email us, we use your address and message to respond and keep correspondence as necessary. These operations serve website delivery, security and responding to enquiries (legitimate interests, or pre-contractual steps where appropriate).

Optional audience measurement

Only after you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited/Google group) receives pseudonymous cookie identifiers, page URLs, visit source, approximate country, device/browser information and interactions such as page views, scrolling, external clicks, downloads and compatible video events. We use this to measure and improve the website, based on your consent. We do not enable Google Signals, User-ID or advertising personalization, and we do not ask for your name or email in Analytics. Refusing does not restrict access. Individual event and user data follow the limited retention configured in Analytics (up to 14 months if the service does not allow a shorter user-data period); aggregated reports can remain longer. No Analytics is installed on privacy-policy pages.

Cookies and choice

The first-party cookies _ga and _ga_X1LNTFZR2N distinguish browsers and sessions. The site requests a 60-day lifetime without renewal on each visit. The localStorage key pe_web_privacy_v1 records your choice and its date for 180 days; it is necessary to remember your preference. Neither Analytics cookies nor its script are loaded before acceptance. Use Cookie settings in the footer to accept, refuse or withdraw. Withdrawal blocks further Analytics collection, deletes accessible Analytics cookies and may reload the page; it does not retroactively erase data already sent. You may also remove cookies/site data in your browser.

External videos and links

YouTube embedded videos are optional and load only after a separate confirmation. Google/YouTube then receives connection and playback data and may use cookies or similar technologies for its own purposes, including advertising. Stop external video removes the player; you can also clear third-party site data via your browser. External links lead to services with their own policies. Accepting website analytics does not authorize YouTube, and vice versa.

Recipients, transfers and rights

Service providers include GitHub and Google. Data may be processed outside the EEA, including the US; providers describe applicable safeguards such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses in their privacy notices. You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object where applicable, and withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing. Contact the controller above. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
