AITHERAXIS: KATABASIS

A single-player sci-fi shoot 'em up where the battlefield itself shifts. In development for PC and consoles. Coming 2027.

Discover the game Contact the studio

The battlefield shifts

Side-scrolling one moment, top-down the next. Energy tunnels fold the camera in the middle of a fight, so a single stage can play like several different games.

Read the bullet patterns, roll through enemies, dodge at the last second and keep your laser from overheating. Then the world turns ninety degrees.

Ten sectors down

Katabasis means descent. Iason Vale, a pilot from Earth, crashes into Akryon, a galaxy full of dead civilizations. Guided only by LYRA, his ship's AI, he has to fight his way through ten sectors, each one guarded by a Custodian.

The eight lights

“When the eight lights gather again, the way to him will open.”

Each Custodian guards a Squixi. Every one you free lights up a new gem in your ship's circuit, and gives your ship a new form. Nobody has told Iason who “him” is.

What you'll do

Fight ten multi-phase bosses
Every sector ends with a Custodian that changes its attacks as it breaks apart.
Survive cinematic set pieces
Quantum tunnels, a fall into a rift and a portal crossing in full 3D, with quick-time moments.
Master a ship that hits back
Attack barrel rolls that cut through enemies, last-second dodges and a laser you have to keep cool.
Follow the story in comic pages
An animated comic prologue and LYRA's logs before each sector tell what happened, and what is still hidden.

One developer, one galaxy

PE Studio is an independent game studio based in Spain. It is run by one person, Diego Ceballos Mora, a computer engineer who designs, codes and builds every part of his games himself.

AITHERAXIS: KATABASIS is its first game.
