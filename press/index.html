Description

AITHERAXIS: KATABASIS is a single-player sci-fi shoot 'em up built around one idea: the battlefield itself shifts. Energy tunnels fold the camera in the middle of a fight, so a single stage moves between side-scrolling, top-down and short 3D sequences.

Players descend through ten hand-crafted sectors, each ending with a Custodian: a multi-phase boss that changes its attacks as it breaks apart. Between sectors, an animated comic prologue and the logs of LYRA, the ship's AI, tell the story of Iason, stranded under a sky full of stars he doesn't recognise.

The game also includes SQUADRON, a local split-screen mode for 1 to 4 players with three modes, three arenas and 22 ships.

Key features

  • Real-time shifts between side-scrolling, top-down and 3D.
  • 10 stages and 10 multi-phase bosses.
  • Attack barrel rolls, last-second dodges and a laser you have to keep cool.
  • A ship that evolves through nine forms.
  • A story told in comic pages.
  • SQUADRON: Battle, Colossus Hunt and Siege for up to 4 players.

About PE Studio
